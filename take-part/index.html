How it works

What actually happens after you get in touch about being a guest.

  1. 1

    A chat, no strings

    A call or a coffee first. We talk about what you'd want to say and what you'd rather leave out. If it isn't the right time for you, that's a fine answer.

  2. 2

    You set the boundaries

    Names, places, people still in your life — anything off limits stays off limits. First names only if you prefer, and we can keep the specifics vague.

  3. 3

    We record

    One conversation, usually an hour, taken at your pace. Stop whenever you like, come back to something, or start a sentence again — that all gets tidied up.

  4. 4

    You hear it before anyone else

    You get the edit before it goes out. If you want a section cut, it's cut. Nothing is published without your yes.
