Help, shared

Mutual aid for the moments that ask a lot of us.

Free, confidential support for mental health, recovery, safety, housing and day-to-day pressures. You do not have to work it all out alone.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. For urgent mental health help that is not an emergency, call NHS 111 and select the mental health option.
