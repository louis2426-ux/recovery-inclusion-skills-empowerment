R.I.S.E Music

Some things are easier to say in a verse than in a conversation. R.I.S.E Music is where the community's own sounds live — tracks, bars and beats written and recorded by people finding their way forward, telling it how it is in their own style and their own words.

Write·Record·Share·Rise

Music as part of recovery

Putting feelings into words and rhythm is a skill, and like any skill it builds confidence.

Your words, your style

Rap, grime, acoustic, spoken word, whatever it is — if it's honest, it's welcome. No one's going to tidy your voice into something it isn't.

Learn the craft

Writing, recording, mixing, putting a track out — picking up real skills along the way is half the point.

Heard by the community

Tracks from R.I.S.E Music get shared alongside the podcast, so they reach the people who need to hear them.

Got a track, or want to make one?

Send us a link, a voice note or just an idea. Whether you've got something finished or you've never recorded before, get in touch and we'll take it from there.
