Support & Connection

Nobody rises alone. If something in an episode hit close to home, or you're just having a hard time, here are people you can talk to — free, confidential, and there for exactly this.

Need help right now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent medical help that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111. Samaritans are there day or night, free from any phone, on 116 123.

Someone to talk to

Free, confidential listening services across the UK.

  • Samaritans

    Talk about anything that's troubling you, any time of day or night.

    116 123

  • Shout

    Free, 24/7 text support if you'd rather not talk out loud.

    Text SHOUT to 85258

  • CALM

    Campaign Against Living Miserably — helpline and webchat, 5pm to midnight every day.

    0800 58 58 58

  • Mind

    Information and support for anyone experiencing a mental health problem.

    mind.org.uk

Drugs, alcohol & recovery

Honest, non-judgemental help wherever you are on the path.

  • FRANK

    Friendly, confidential drugs advice, 24 hours a day.

    0300 123 6600

  • Drinkline

    Free, confidential helpline for anyone worried about their own or someone else's drinking.

    0300 123 1110

  • Narcotics Anonymous UK

    Peer support meetings for people recovering from drug addiction.

    ukna.org

  • Alcoholics Anonymous GB

    Find a local or online meeting, or call the free helpline.

    0800 9177 650
